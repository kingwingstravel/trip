Good to know before you fly

Food: please read

Kazakhstan is a meat-loving country, and vegetarian choices are limited in many places, especially outside Almaty. That is why we include one pre-booked lunch every day at places that serve vegetarian and Indian-style food.

New Year week is the busiest time of the year: many restaurants are fully booked. We strongly recommend letting us reserve your dinners too, especially on 31 December.

Winter weather

Expect about −5 °C in the city and down to −15 °C or colder in the mountains and at Kolsai. Warm layers, snow boots, gloves, a hat, sunglasses and sun cream will make every day comfortable.

Mountains decide

Cable cars and mountain roads depend on the weather. The road to Kaindy is driven by 4×4. If a site is closed for safety, we replace it with the best alternative on the day — for example, we drive up Kok-Tobe by car.

Holidays and crowds

1 and 2 January are public holidays in Kazakhstan. Shymbulak and the city centre are busy, so we start early where it helps.

Late flight home

Your flight leaves at 03:05 on 6 January. After check-out on 5 January your luggage stays with the hotel, and we reach the airport around midnight.

Money and phone

Cards are accepted almost everywhere in Almaty. Carry some tenge in cash for villages and small shops. Mobile signal can be weak at Kolsai and Kaindy.
