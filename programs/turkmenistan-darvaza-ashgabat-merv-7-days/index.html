Turkmenistan 7 Days: Kunya-Urgench, Darvaza Gas Crater, Ashgabat and Merv

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Khiva – Shavat border – Kunya-Urgench – Dashoguz

    Morning transfer from Khiva to the Shavat border and crossing into Turkmenistan, where the Turkmen guide meets the group. Kunya-Urgench (UNESCO): mausoleums of Fakhr ad-Din, Sultan Tekesh, Turabek Khanum and Najm ad-Din Kubra, and the Kutlug Timur Minaret. Drive to Dashoguz and hotel check-in.

  2. Day 2

    Dashoguz – Darvaza gas crater

    Drive south across the Karakum Desert to Darvaza (about 4–5 hours). Check in at the yurt camp and walk to the burning gas crater at sunset and after dark. Night in the yurt camp.

  3. Day 3

    Darvaza – Ashgabat: Nisa and the National Museum

    Drive to Ashgabat (about 4 hours). Visit Nisa, the Parthian capital and a UNESCO site, and the National Museum of Turkmenistan (closed on Tuesdays and national holidays). Hotel check-in.

  4. Day 4

    Ashgabat city tour

    Full day in Ashgabat: Ertugrul Gazi Mosque, the Independence Monument with the statues of great Turkmen, the Neutrality Arch, the Turkmenbashy Ruhy Mosque and the medieval town of Anau with the ruins of the Seyit Jemaleddin Mosque.

  5. Day 5

    Ashgabat – Mary: Ancient Merv

    Drive to Mary (320 km, about 3.5 hours). Ancient Merv (UNESCO): Big and Small Kyz Kala, Erk Kala and Gyaur Kala, the Sultan Sanjar Mausoleum, the Ashab complex and the sardoba water cistern. Hotel check-in.

  6. Day 6

    Mary – Ashgabat

    Morning at the central bazaar of Mary and the Orthodox church. Drive back to Ashgabat on the new highway. Afternoon: Halk Hakydasy memorial (outside), the Alem Ferris wheel, the Lenin monument and the Berkarar shopping centre.

  7. Day 7

    Departure from Ashgabat

    Breakfast and transfer to Ashgabat airport for the flight home.

When is the best time to travel?

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are the most comfortable. From December to March the mountains near Almaty are snowy, and some mountain lakes and passes can be closed.
