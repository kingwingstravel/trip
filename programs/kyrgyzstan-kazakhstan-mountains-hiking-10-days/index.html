Tian Shan Mountains: Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan Hiking, 10 Days

Ala-Archa, a yurt night on Issyk-Kul, Skazka Canyon, Barskoon waterfalls, the red rocks of Jeti-Oguz, the hot springs of Altyn-Arashan, Kolsai and Kaindy lakes, Charyn Canyon, Big Almaty Lake and a night at an eco hotel above Shymbulak. Walks of 2–4 hours on most days.

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Bishkek

    Meet and greet at Bishkek airport, transfer and hotel check-in.

  2. Day 2

    Ala-Archa – Issyk-Kul

    Hike in Ala-Archa National Park among glaciers and juniper forest. Drive to the south shore of Issyk-Kul and check in at a yurt camp.

  3. Day 3

    Skazka Canyon – Barskoon waterfalls – Jeti-Oguz – Karakol

    Skazka (Fairy Tale) Canyon with its red and orange rock shapes and an eagle hunting show. Walk to the Barskoon waterfalls in a mountain gorge. Jeti-Oguz gorge: the red Seven Bulls rocks and the Broken Heart cliff. Evening in Karakol.

  4. Day 4

    Altyn-Arashan

    Mountain vehicle up the valley to Altyn-Arashan. Meadow hiking and natural hot springs at 2,600 m.

  5. Day 5

    Karkara border – Kolsai

    Descend by mountain vehicle, cross the Karkara–Kegen border into Kazakhstan and continue to the Kolsai area. Kaindy Lake on the way if time allows.

  6. Day 6

    Kolsai – Charyn – Almaty

    Morning walk at Kolsai Lake, then Charyn Canyon and the Valley of Castles. Drive to Almaty.

  7. Day 7

    Big Almaty Lake

    Drive up to Big Almaty Lake for walking and free time. Sunset at Kok-Tobe hill.

  8. Day 8

    Shymbulak

    Cable car to Shymbulak and a 2–3 hour mountain hike. Night at an eco hotel in the mountains.

  9. Day 9

    Shymbulak – Almaty

    Cable car down, city walk, shopping and farewell dinner. Evening transfer to the airport.

  10. Day 10

    Departure

    Night flight from Almaty.

Questions agents ask

Do guests need a visa?

It depends on the passport; citizens of many countries, including the EU, UK and Turkey, can enter Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan visa-free for short stays. We check the entry rules for your guests' passports with every quotation.

How hard are the walks?

Easy to moderate. Most walks are 2–5 hours on paths with no climbing, and a 4×4 or a horse can replace some of them. Guests should be fit enough to walk a few hours in the mountains. The highest points are about 2,500 m in Kazakhstan and up to about 3,500 m in Kyrgyzstan.

What should guests bring?

Good walking shoes, a warm jacket and layers (mountain evenings are cold even in summer), sunglasses, sun cream, a hat and a small backpack. In yurt camps a torch is useful.

Can we change the program?

Yes. Every program can be adjusted: number of days, hotels, meals, extra cities or another Central Asian country.

What group size do you work with?

These programs are planned for groups of 10 to 40 guests. Smaller groups and private tours on request.

How do agents pay?

Deposit 30% when the booking is confirmed, the balance 30 days before arrival, by bank transfer in USD. Regular partners can agree other options.

What if the group cancels?

31 days or more before arrival you lose only the deposit; 15–30 days before, 50%; 14 days or less, 100%.

When does this program run?

This program runs June–September. July and August are the warmest months; June and September are quieter. Mountain roads and passes depend on the weather, and we confirm the route for every group before arrival.
