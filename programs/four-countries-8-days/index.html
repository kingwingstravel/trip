Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Tashkent

    Evening arrival at Tashkent International Airport. Meet and greet, transfer and hotel check-in.

  2. Day 2

    Tashkent – Almaty

    Morning flight to Almaty. Afternoon city tour: Panfilov Park, Zenkov Cathedral, Republic Square, Green Bazaar, Kok-Tobe hill by cable car and the Medeu viewpoint.

  3. Day 3

    Almaty – Bishkek by road

    About 235 km (4–5 hours) via the Korday border. Lunch on the way. Ala-Too Square and Osh Bazaar in Bishkek.

  4. Day 4

    Ala-Archa – Tashkent

    Morning in Ala-Archa National Park. Afternoon flight from Bishkek to Tashkent and hotel check-in.

  5. Day 5

    Tashkent – Samarkand

    Afrosiyob high-speed train to Samarkand. Gur-Emir Mausoleum, Registan Square and Bibi-Khanym Mosque.

  6. Day 6

    Day trip to Tajikistan

    Cross the Jartepa border to Panjakent: the Seven Lakes (Haft Kul), the local bazaar and the Rudaki Museum. Return to Samarkand.

  7. Day 7

    Samarkand – Tashkent

    Shah-i-Zinda, Ulugbek Observatory, the Konigil paper-making village and Siyob Bazaar. Evening Afrosiyob train to Tashkent; last night in a 5-star hotel.

  8. Day 8

    Tashkent and departure

    Khast Imam Complex, Chorsu Bazaar and the Tashkent Metro. Farewell dinner in a private dining room, then transfer to the airport.

Is the food halal?

Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan are Muslim-majority countries. For Muslim groups we arrange halal restaurants for every meal we book.

Can we change the program?

Yes. Every program can be adjusted: number of days, hotels, meals, extra cities or another Central Asian country.

What group size do you work with?

These programs are planned for groups of 10 to 40 guests. Smaller groups and private tours on request.

How do agents pay?

Deposit 30% when the booking is confirmed, the balance 30 days before arrival, by bank transfer in USD. Regular partners can agree other options.

What if the group cancels?

31 days or more before arrival you lose only the deposit; 15–30 days before, 50%; 14 days or less, 100%.

When is the best time to travel?

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are the most comfortable. From December to March the mountains near Almaty are snowy, and some mountain lakes and passes can be closed.
